How It Works

We don't change anything.
We just digitise what you already do.

Your stokvel and burial society already have trust, community, and processes. Beyond-Score brings those into the digital age — without adding complexity or cost.

01

Available Offline

No internet? No problem. Beyond-Score works fully offline, syncing data when connectivity is available. Your community never waits.

02

USSD Integration

Access your score and update records via simple USSD codes from any phone — no smartphone needed. Inclusive by design.

03

Online Dashboard

For those who prefer digital, our dashboard gives you real-time visibility into scores, trends, and member activity.

04

No Changes, No Charges

We don't restructure your society. We don't charge your members. We simply digitise your existing practices and trust.

Your community score, in your pocket.

Our Progressive Web App puts your stokvel's credit score at your fingertips — fast, lightweight, and accessible.
