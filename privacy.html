3. How We Collect Your Information

We collect information in the following ways:

3.1 Directly From You

  • Registration: When you create an account as a member or leader.
  • Contributions: When you submit weekly/monthly contribution entries.
  • Verification: When leaders verify member contributions.
  • Profile Updates: When you update your personal or group information.
  • Communications: When you contact us via email, WhatsApp, or USSD.

3.2 Automatically Through the Platform

  • Offline-first architecture: Data is stored locally on your device and synced when you're online. This allows you to use the Platform even without internet.
  • USSD sessions: When you use our USSD service, we collect your phone number and session data to process your requests.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: We use minimal cookies to maintain session state and remember your preferences.

5. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

5.1 Core Platform Functions

  • Create and manage your stokvel or burial society group.
  • Record, store, and verify contribution history.
  • Calculate and display your Beyondscore.
  • Enable leaders to verify member contributions with one tap.
  • Provide USSD access for feature phone users.
  • Sync offline entries when you reconnect to the internet.

5.2 Marketplace & Partner Services

  • Show you relevant offers from partners (groceries, fuel, insurance, lenders).
  • Connect you with financial products you qualify for based on your score.
  • Facilitate referrals to trusted partners (with your explicit opt-in).

5.3 Platform Improvement

  • Analyse usage patterns to improve user experience.
  • Identify and fix bugs, security issues, and performance problems.
  • Develop new features based on user behaviour and feedback.

5.4 Security & Fraud Prevention

  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity, identity theft, and unauthorised access.
  • Verify identity to prevent impersonation and fake entries.
  • Maintain audit logs for compliance and dispute resolution.

5.5 Legal & Regulatory Compliance

  • Comply with POPIA, the National Credit Act, and other applicable laws.
  • Respond to lawful requests from law enforcement, courts, and regulatory bodies.
  • Maintain records required by financial regulations.

We retain your personal information for as long as you have an active account with Beyondscore. After you delete your account, we retain certain data for the following periods:

9. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

9.1 Technical Safeguards

  • End-to-end encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Offline-first architecture: Sensitive data is stored locally on your device and is encrypted.
  • Access controls: Only authorised personnel can access data, and only for specific purposes.
  • Audit logging: All access to sensitive data is logged and regularly reviewed.

9.2 Organisational Safeguards

  • POPIA compliance officer: We have designated an Information Officer responsible for data protection.
  • Staff training: All personnel handling data are trained on POPIA and data protection.
  • Incident response: We have a breach notification procedure to notify affected parties and the Information Regulator within 72 hours as required by POPIA.

🔐 Security Incident: In the event of a data breach that may compromise your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by POPIA.

Beyondscore is a South African platform, and all data is stored on servers located in South Africa (AWS South Africa region). We do not routinely transfer your data outside the country.

Exceptions: If we use third-party services (e.g., USSD gateway, WhatsApp API) that may process data outside South Africa, we ensure that:

12. Specific Provisions for Stokvel & Burial Society Data

12.1 Community Attestation

Beyondscore relies on community attestation to verify contribution records. When a leader verifies a member's contribution, that verification is recorded and used to build the member's score.

  • Leaders: Are responsible for verifying contributions of members in their group.
  • Members: Consent to having their contributions shared with their group leader for verification purposes.
  • Verification records: Are stored permanently for audit and dispute resolution.

12.2 Historical Contribution Data

If your stokvel or burial society submits historical contribution records, we treat these with the same level of protection as current data. Historical records are used to:

  • Calculate an initial Beyondscore for members.
  • Provide a longer financial history for better score accuracy.
  • Establish the group's reliability and stability.

12.3 Group Data Rights

  • Group leaders can view and verify member contributions within their group.
  • Members can view their own contributions and score.
  • Members cannot see other members' individual contribution data — only aggregated group data (e.g., "8 of 12 members verified").
